How fit is decided

Fits
The listing asks for 4 years or fewer. If it doesn't say, the title is Senior, Product Designer or Designer II.
Apply anyway
The listing asks for 5–6 years, or the title is Lead with no experience given. Companies often stretch by a year for a strong portfolio.
Stretch
The listing asks for 7+ years, or the title is Staff or Principal.
Hidden
Listings whose experience range misses 3–4 years (5+ minimum, or under 3 maximum) or whose stated pay tops out below 15 LPA. Ranges that reach 3–4 years, unstated experience and unstated pay stay visible.
